League Rules

Standings Race Results Rules

Race Schedule

  • Track is hot at 4:00 PM for testing.
  • Racers will be paired up at 7:00 PM for first round. Buy-in is $20.
  • First round will begin at 7:30 PM.
  • Once first round is completed, there will be a 20 minute break.
  • First-round losers can buy back in for $10 and will be paired up for second round.
  • Pairing for second round will take place immediately following first round.
  • After second round, there is a 20 minute break to prep for third round.
  • After third round, there is a 20 minute break to prep for the final round.
  • Finals are run after the third-round break.
  • $20 from the race winner’s pot will be put into the pot for the championship at the end of the year.

Car Rules

  • Car must be 1/10 scale.
  • Two-wheel drive cars only, with rubber slicks on the rear.
  • Car must have four wheels.
  • Cars must have a full body that covers all four wheels — no open-wheel bodies.
  • Fully dressed car must weigh a minimum of 2250 grams.
  • Wheelbase must be no less than 13 inches and no more than 14 inches.
  • Simple Green is the only type of tire prep allowed. No other compounds or additives.
  • Tire heaters are allowed.
  • 2-cell or 3-cell batteries are allowed.

Race Rules

  • Once a racer has staged, his/her competitor has 7 seconds to stage or be DQ’d.
  • The first car to cross the finish line in his/her lane wins the race.
  • If a racer leaves early and lights the red light on the tree, he/she is DQ’d.
  • If both racers red-light, the racer who left first is DQ’d.
  • If a racer hits any of the timing equipment, he/she is DQ’d.
  • If a racer crosses the centerline and interferes with the other racer, he/she is DQ’d.

Double Mukidy Fuck

In the event that both racers crash into the timing system, the race will be re-run. Both racers will be allowed to buy back in if this happens in the first round.

Rules Regarding Points (Next Season)

  • Attending a race will earn you 20 points.
  • Each round win is worth 20 points.
  • A race win is worth 40 points.
  • To qualify for the championship at the end of the year, you must have at least 720 points.
